## Data

### 1. Source data

- `Source_Data.xlsx`: participant-level data underlying the figures and Tables 1–2.

### 2. Behavioral data

- `behavior_trial_accuracy.csv`: trial-level accuracy for each memory measure.

Participant IDs match those in `Source_Data.xlsx`.

### 3. Neuroimaging data

- `neuroimaging_maps.zip`: unthresholded group-level T- and F-statistic maps for Figs. 3–6 and Supplementary Figs. 7 and 13.

Maps are organized into `activation` and `connectivity` folders. EA and noEA indicate participants with and without emotional abuse, respectively. Positive T-values for group differences and interactions indicate larger effects or slopes in the noEA group.
